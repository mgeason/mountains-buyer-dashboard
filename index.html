How the calculations work

Budget routing uses an existing studio when explicitly recorded; its approval status remains a separate check. Without a studio, the $850k path requires recorded evidence of usable building space. Land size alone never confirms that space.

Campaign age runs from the latest listed/relisted date to sale, withdrawal, or today in Sydney. Cumulative advertised time sums known campaign intervals, excluding withdrawn gaps. Missing starts and endpoints are reported as unknown.

Price changes compare dated guide events in the current campaign. A same-price content update is not a price cut. Sale-to-guide percentages require a numeric guide and sale price. With a guide range, endpoints are retained.

Quarterly medians and sales counts use sale date, not the date the record was entered. Unknown sale dates are excluded. No sale is projected into a later quarter.
