How to interpret the evidence

An advertised studio does not establish approved secondary dwelling use. An 800 m² block does not establish usable building space. Record approval documents, slope, access, site layout and constraints separately.

Campaign age uses the latest listed/relisted event to sale, withdrawal or today in Sydney. Cumulative advertised time excludes known withdrawn gaps and remains unknown when prior campaigns are missing. Original guide requires a numeric guide recorded with the start of the current campaign; a later observation is not the original asking price.

Guide ranges use midpoints for summary percentages, with endpoints in details. Quarterly medians use sale date and include sample counts. Unknown sale dates are excluded from trend charts. The changing mix of homes sold can change a median without equivalent properties changing value.
